Short bilingual brief and strategy summary derived from the main business plan and strategy blueprint, intended for quick sharing with collaborators or early investor conversations.

这是一份基于主业务计划与策略蓝图整理的双语简报与策略摘要，适合用于与合作方及早期投资人进行高效沟通。

## 1. Concept / 核心概念

An AI-native hedge fund is not a traditional fund with AI tools added on top. It is an investment firm built from the ground up around AI-assisted research synthesis, portfolio intelligence, and risk-aware decision support.

AI 原生对冲基金，并不是在传统基金之上简单叠加 AI 工具，而是从底层架构开始，就围绕 AI 辅助的研究整合、组合洞察与风险驱动决策支持来打造的投资机构。

---

## 2. The Core Belief / 核心判断

The next generation of hedge funds will compete not only on people and capital, but on the quality of their internal intelligence systems.

下一代对冲基金的竞争力，不仅取决于人才与资本，更取决于其内部智能系统的质量。

Small teams can now operate with much broader research coverage because software and AI agents can absorb a large share of the information-processing workload.

如今，小团队之所以能够实现更广的研究覆盖，核心在于软件与 AI Agent 已能够承担相当大比例的信息处理工作。

---

## 3. The Problem / 行业痛点

Traditional investment workflows are fragmented. Research lives across terminals, notes, chats, and PDFs. Risk is often reviewed too late. Scaling insight usually means hiring more people.

传统投资流程往往较为割裂。研究信息散落在终端、笔记、聊天记录和 PDF 之间，风险识别通常偏慢，而研究能力的扩张也常常只能依赖增加人手。

This makes the process slower, more expensive, and harder to improve.

这会导致投资流程效率下降、运营成本上升，也使流程优化变得更困难。

---

## 4. Our Solution / 我们的方案

We are building a proprietary investment operating system that unifies:

我们正在构建一套专有的投资操作系统，将以下核心能力整合到同一框架中：

- portfolio monitoring and exposure analysis
- 组合监控与敞口分析
- multi-timeframe market data infrastructure
- 多周期市场数据基础设施
- technical and volatility analytics
- 技术分析与波动率分析
- AI-driven news and macro intelligence
- AI 驱动的新闻与宏观情报
- AI-assisted portfolio advisory and screening
- AI 辅助的组合建议与机会筛选
- repeatable reporting and review workflows
- 可重复的报告与复盘流程

---

## 5. What Makes It AI-Native / 为什么它是 AI 原生

AI is embedded in the daily operating loop, not used as a side tool.

AI 被嵌入到日常投资运作的核心流程之中，而不是作为边缘性的辅助工具存在。

The system continuously ingests data, classifies information, monitors exposures, ranks opportunities, and generates structured recommendations for the portfolio manager.

系统能够持续采集数据、归类信息、监控组合敞口、识别并排序机会，并向投资经理输出结构化建议。

Human judgment remains responsible for final capital allocation and risk ownership.

最终的资本配置决策与风险承担责任，仍然由人来掌握。

---

## 6. Investment Style / 投资风格

The initial strategy is best described as AI-amplified discretionary investing in liquid public markets, especially equities, options, and thematic baskets.

初始策略更适合被定义为 AI 增强的主观型公开市场投资，重点覆盖股票、期权以及主题篮子。

The platform combines macro context, portfolio context, event awareness, technical confirmation, and options-aware risk management.

平台将宏观环境、组合状态、事件催化、技术确认以及期权风险管理有机结合在一起。

---

## 7. Target Universe / 目标范围

The initial opportunity set should stay focused on liquid public markets:

初始机会集应聚焦于高流动性的公开市场：

- large-cap and liquid mid-cap U.S. equities
- 美国大盘股及高流动性中盘股
- index and single-name options with tradable liquidity
- 具备良好交易流动性的指数期权与个股期权
- thematic baskets linked to sectors, macro trends, or catalysts
- 围绕行业、宏观趋势或事件催化构建的主题篮子

The early strategy should avoid illiquid securities, overly complex structures, and markets where data or execution quality is weak.

在早期阶段，策略应避免低流动性证券、过于复杂的结构性产品，以及在数据质量或执行把握上存在明显短板的市场。

## 8. Sources of Edge / 优势来源

The strategy does not depend on one magic signal. It depends on better information processing.

这一策略并不依赖某一个“神奇信号”，而是依赖更高效、更系统化的信息处理能力。

- faster synthesis of market, portfolio, and research inputs
- 更快整合市场、组合与研究输入
- better prioritization of catalysts, setups, and risk
- 更有效地对催化、机会与风险进行优先级排序
- better portfolio context when evaluating new ideas
- 在评估新机会时具备更完整的组合上下文
- better feedback loops from recommendations, actions, and outcomes
- 从建议、执行与结果中形成更完整的反馈闭环

## 9. Strategy Archetypes / 策略原型

The operating system is best suited to a few repeatable trade archetypes:

这套操作系统最适合支撑少数几类可重复、可迭代的交易原型：

### A. Catalyst-aware directional trades / 事件驱动方向性交易

Build or reduce equity exposure around macro shifts, earnings, Fed events, or major news.

围绕宏观变化、财报、联储事件或重大新闻，建立或降低股票方向性敞口。

### B. Options expression and hedging / 期权表达与对冲

Use options when they improve convexity, define risk more clearly, or hedge concentrated positions.

当期权能够提升收益凸性、更清晰地限定风险，或对冲集中仓位时，优先使用期权表达观点。

### C. Basket-level thematic positioning / 篮子级主题配置

Express views through groups of related names such as AI infrastructure, semiconductors, biotech, or macro-sensitive sectors.

通过一组相关标的来表达主题观点，例如 AI 基础设施、半导体、生物科技或宏观敏感板块。

### D. Portfolio reshaping and de-risking / 组合重塑与降风险

Let the platform identify crowding, imbalance, or regime mismatch, then rebalance or hedge.

由平台识别拥挤交易、组合失衡或与市场环境不匹配的风险，再推动再平衡或对冲动作。

## 10. Portfolio Construction / 组合构建

The portfolio should begin with simple, explainable rules:

组合在起步阶段应遵循简单、透明且可解释的规则：

- prefer liquid instruments
- 优先配置高流动性工具
- size positions by conviction and liquidity
- 根据观点强度与流动性条件决定仓位大小
- monitor single-name, sector, and basket concentration
- 持续监控单一个股、板块及主题篮子的集中度
- use options selectively rather than by default
- 谨慎、选择性地使用期权，而不是默认使用
- preserve cash and hedge capacity for regime shifts
- 为市场环境切换保留充足的现金与对冲能力

A practical starting structure is a core book, a tactical overlay, and an explicit hedge layer.

一个务实的初始结构可以包括：核心仓位层、战术交易层，以及明确的对冲层。

## 12. Decision Loop / 决策闭环

The strategy should operate as a repeatable loop:

策略应以一个可重复、可复盘的闭环方式运行：

1. News and macro agent flags regime shifts or catalysts.
2. Portfolio advisor identifies concentration or exposure mismatch.
3. Screener agent finds candidate adds, replacements, or hedges.
4. Portfolio manager reviews trade-offs and makes the decision.
5. Daily reporting records the signal, action, and resulting exposure.

1. 新闻与宏观 Agent 识别市场环境变化或关键催化。
2. 组合顾问 Agent 识别集中风险或敞口错配。
3. 筛选 Agent 生成新增、替换或对冲候选。
4. 投资经理权衡取舍后做出最终决策。
5. 每日报告记录信号、动作以及结果敞口。

## 14. Risk Philosophy / 风险理念

AI is used for summarization, classification, screening, and recommendation generation.

AI 主要用于信息总结、分类、筛选以及建议生成。

AI is not used for unsupervised capital deployment or bypassing portfolio controls.

AI 不用于无人监督的资金部署，也不会绕开组合风控体系。

The platform is designed to automate awareness, not automate recklessness.

平台要自动化的是“感知与认知能力”，而不是“冒进式决策”。

## 15. Formation Path / 发展路径

### Phase 1 / 第一阶段

Internal capital incubation using the platform as the daily operating core.

以自有或关联资金进行孵化，验证平台作为日常投资操作核心的可行性。

### Phase 2 / 第二阶段

Small external capital through a controlled structure such as friends-and-family or SMA.

通过 friends-and-family、SMA 等相对可控的结构，引入小规模外部资金。

### Phase 3 / 第三阶段

Institutionalization with stronger controls, reporting, and allocator-ready positioning.

在更完善的风控、报告体系与募资叙事基础上，逐步走向机构化。
