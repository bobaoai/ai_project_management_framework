## 2. One-Slide Thesis

### Core message

The next generation of hedge funds will not simply use AI tools. They will be built around AI as their operating system.

### Key points

- Traditional funds are constrained by human processing bandwidth.
- Our platform converts research, risk, and signal review into a continuous machine-assisted workflow.
- Human judgment remains in control of capital allocation.

---

## 3. The Problem

### Investor-facing framing

Most investment teams are overwhelmed by information but underpowered in synthesis.

### Proof points

- Research is fragmented across too many tools and formats.
- Portfolio risk is often reviewed after information has already moved.
- Scaling research usually requires scaling headcount.
- Process quality is difficult to audit and improve.

---

## 4. Why Now

### Market timing

Three technology shifts now make an AI-native hedge fund practical:

1. Brokerage and market-data APIs allow direct portfolio and price integration.
2. Modern LLMs can structure unstructured financial information at usable cost.
3. Small teams can now build institutional-quality internal tooling.

---

## 5. Our Solution

### Positioning statement

We are building a hedge fund whose core operating layer is proprietary software plus AI agents.

### System functions

- live portfolio and exposure monitoring
- multi-timeframe market data storage
- technical and volatility analytics
- AI-driven news and macro triage
- AI-assisted portfolio review and idea screening
- repeatable reporting and attribution

---

## 6. Product Architecture

### Suggested visual

Use a simple pipeline diagram:

`Data Inputs -> Analytics Engine -> AI Agents -> Portfolio Manager -> Reporting / Feedback Loop`

### Talking points

- Inputs include brokerage positions, historical prices, options data, and research feeds.
- The analytics layer structures market state and portfolio risk.
- The agent layer translates inputs into ranked recommendations.
- The output is decision support, not autonomous trading.

---

## 7. What Makes It AI-Native

### Distinction

This is not a traditional discretionary fund with a chatbot on top.

### AI-native attributes

- AI is embedded in the daily operating loop.
- Recommendations are tied to live positions and exposures.
- Research synthesis happens continuously, not episodically.
- Internal process data compounds into a proprietary training and review asset.

---

## 8. Investment Style

### Strategy description

AI-amplified discretionary investing in liquid public markets, especially equities, options, and thematic baskets.

### Inputs combined

- macro regime context
- portfolio exposure context
- catalyst and news context
- technical confirmation across timeframes
- options-aware risk framing

---

## 9. Edge

### Structural edge

The edge is the operating model, not a single factor or signal.

### Supporting points

- more information processed per day at lower marginal cost
- tighter feedback loops between research and risk
- persistent memory of recommendations, decisions, and outcomes
- institutional process depth without institutional overhead

---

## 10. Risk Controls

### Main message

AI improves awareness, not recklessness.

### Explicit boundaries

- AI assists with summarization, screening, and recommendation generation.
- Human oversight remains responsible for allocation and controls.
- Exposure visibility, concentration review, and options-risk monitoring are built into the platform.
